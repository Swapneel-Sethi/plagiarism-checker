    About

    Here's how it works. The checker slides a 5-word window across your writing and checks each chunk against the reference texts. If a chunk shows up word-for-word, that's a verbatim match. If it's reworded but means the same thing, the engine catches that too with a semantic pass. It also looks for ten signs of AI writing, such as low vocabulary diversity or repetitive word length, to estimate how machine-generated your writing sounds.

    With web scanning on, it also checks your text against live Google results through the backend. This is a rough heuristic, not a certified plagiarism or AI detector. Think of it as a second pair of eyes, not a court of law.
