    About

    Here's how it works. The checker slides a 5-word window across your writing and checks each chunk against the reference texts below. If a chunk shows up word-for-word, that's a verbatim match. If it's reworded but means the same thing, the engine catches that too with a semantic pass (TF-IDF cosine similarity). It also runs your prose through ten stylometric dials — burstiness, rare-word ratio, function-word density, boilerplate phrasing, LLM tells, personal-voice absence, repeated n-grams, vocabulary diversity, word length, and punctuation structure — to estimate how machine-generated your writing sounds.

    With web scanning on, it also checks your text against live Google results through the backend — on the deployed site the search key is already set up. This is a rough heuristic, not a certified plagiarism or AI detector. Think of it as a second pair of eyes, not a court of law.
