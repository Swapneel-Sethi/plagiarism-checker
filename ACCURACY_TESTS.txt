=== SAMPLE 1: [PLAG] Paraphrase of Wikipedia "Photosynthesis" ===
Plants and many other living things use a process called photosynthesis to turn the energy in sunlight into a form of chemical energy they can later use to power their own functions. That chemical energy gets locked away inside sugar and starch molecules built from carbon dioxide and water. In plants this usually depends on the green pigment chlorophyll, and as a side effect the process releases oxygen into the air. Most of the oxygen in Earth's atmosphere was produced this way. The overall reaction captures light, usually from the sun, and uses it to remake simple molecules into ones that store energy. Organisms that do this are called photoautotrophs, and they form the base of almost every food chain on the planet. Without the steady conversion of light into stored energy, most life as we know it could not continue.
=== SAMPLE 2: [PLAG] Paraphrase of Wikipedia "Democracy" ===
Democracy is a form of government in which the people either govern themselves directly or choose representatives who make decisions on their behalf. The word comes from Greek and literally means the rule of the people. In a direct democracy, citizens vote on laws and policies themselves, while in a representative democracy they elect officials to do so. Free and fair elections, the protection of minority rights, and the rule of law are commonly seen as essential features. The idea spread from ancient city-states to become, by the twentieth century, the most widely accepted form of government in the world. Critics note that wealth and influence can distort the process even where the formal machinery is intact.
=== SAMPLE 3: [PLAG] Paraphrase of Wikipedia "Climate change" ===
Climate change describes the long-term warming of the planet and the shifts in weather patterns that go with it, driven chiefly by human activity, above all the burning of coal, oil, and gas. The consequences reach across natural ecosystems and the systems human societies depend on. Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, raising average temperatures and disrupting rainfall, seasons, and coastlines. Scientists link the recent rapid rise in temperature to the industrial release of carbon dioxide since the nineteenth century. Projected effects include more frequent extreme weather, rising seas, and stress on food and water supplies. Efforts to respond focus on cutting emissions and on adapting to changes that can no longer be avoided.
=== SAMPLE 4: [PLAG] Paraphrase of Wikipedia "Black hole" ===
A black hole is a region where gravity is so intense that not even light can break free. The edge of that region is known as the event horizon. They are born when very massive stars run out of fuel and collapse in on themselves. Beyond the horizon, the known laws of physics no longer give a complete description. The first image of a black hole was released in 2019 by a network of radio telescopes working together across the globe. Although they cannot be seen directly, their presence is inferred from the way they bend light and pull on nearby stars and gas. Smaller ones may form from collapsed stars, while the largest, found at the centers of galaxies, can outweigh the sun by billions of times.
